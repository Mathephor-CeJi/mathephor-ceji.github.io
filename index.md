I am currently an assistant professor (lecturer) at Center for Applied Mathematics, Tianjin University. Before I received my B.Sc. and Ph. D. from School of Mathematical Sciences at Peking University under supervision of <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/guoshuaimath/home?authuser=0">Shuai Guo</a>, and was a Postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at Tsinghua University, in the group of <a href="https://math.tsinghua.edu.cn/mathen/info/1125/1369.htm">Youjin Zhang</a>.

I work on mathematical physics, with an emphasis on enumerative geometry, mirror symmetry and integrable systems.

My name in Chinese characters is 季策 (Ji, Ce). For non-native Chinese speakers, please feel free to pronounce my name as you think it should be pronounced. Cedric would also be a good alternative :)
